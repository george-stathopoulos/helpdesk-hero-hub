# Helpdesk Hero Hub

**Tickets with answers attached. One-click access. Your rules.**

The free support hub for agencies, freelancers, plugin vendors and hosts. Your customers install the free [Helpdesk Hero](https://github.com/george-stathopoulos/helpdesk-hero) plugin and connect to your hub with a code. Tickets arrive with diagnostics and a health check, you log in to their site with one click (no passwords), and everything you do is logged for them.

- Inbox with tags, dashboard messages, emailed tickets and statistics
- Support policies and templates: access levels, length of access, what customers can choose, what tickets include
- Signed, direct connections between your hub and each site: no service in the middle
- Backup and restore of the whole hub

**Documentation:** [docs/](docs/README.md) in this repository, with screenshots ([Getting started](docs/getting-started.md), [FAQ](docs/faq.md), [Troubleshooting](docs/troubleshooting.md)).

**Website, help center and live demo:** https://george-stathopoulos.github.io/helpdesk-hero/
